Tips
  • Flatbed — place a single sheet on the glass; ideal for photos and bound documents.
  • ADF Single Sided — feeds a stack of pages through the feeder, scanning one side of each.
  • ADF Both Sides — requires a physical duplexer in the scanner; scans front and back of each sheet automatically.
  • Use 300 DPI for standard documents; 600 DPI for photos or fine print.
  • Set Page Limit to 0 when scanning a full ADF stack of unknown length.
